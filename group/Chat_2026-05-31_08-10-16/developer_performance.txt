[CRITICAL] STREAMING DEATH SPIRAL:
Moi token AI streaming gay ra cascade sau:
  1. chat_chunk socket event (20-50 lan/giay)
  2. setMessages() clone TOAN BO messages array
  3. React re-render ChatPanel, goi renderMarkdown cho MOI message
